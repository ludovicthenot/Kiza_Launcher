Kiza Launcher 0.0.372

## New

**Kiza's loading screen in Minecraft.** While the game starts, the Kiza
emblem and "Kiza Client" rise into the middle of the screen with a line of
progress in your accent colour under them. Once the game has loaded, the
line folds away and the two travel up to their place on the title screen,
where the menu appears around them. Turn it off under Interface in the
client settings. Fabric and Vanilla; Forge and NeoForge keep their own
loading screens.

**A new launcher opening.** The mark and the name arrive one after the other,
a single line says what the launcher is doing, and the whole of it steps
back to show your library once it is ready.

## Fixed

**Discover shows the details beside the list again** whenever there is the
room for both, including with Windows display scaling at 125 or 150 %.

**The colour palette's marker stays in the palette.** Dragged past its
edge, it no longer leaves the square or the strip, and scrolled out of
view, it no longer floats alone in the settings.

**The library no longer covers the settings' title** when the settings are
scrolled.
