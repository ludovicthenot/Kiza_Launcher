Kiza Launcher 0.0.365

A new engine for the menu background: smooth video, sharp pictures.

## New

**Smooth menu video, up to 1440p at 60 fps.** The first time a
video is chosen, Kiza Client prepares it once, on several cores, and keeps
it beside the file; afterwards it plays from that, on its own clock and at
the video's real timing. Frames that would be late are skipped instead of
slowing the video down. Leaving the menus pauses it and stops its threads;
coming back resumes it where it was.

**Video settings in the game.** Under Video in the Kiza Client settings:
quality (Auto, 720p, 1080p, 1440p), smoothness (Auto, 30 or 60 fps), a
performance mode (720p at 30 fps) and on-screen measurements. A change
applies without restarting the game.

**Redesigned client settings.** The panel behind the gear is laid out like
Apple's settings: grouped rows, segmented controls, switches and a thin
slider. It rises from the bottom of the screen and goes back down the same
way.

**The background on every menu**, blurred, outside a world.

## Fixed

**Pixelated videos, GIFs and images.** Backgrounds are now drawn smoothly
instead of in squares, at every Minecraft version, and images keep up to
2560 by 1440 instead of 960 by 540.

**Washed-out video colours.** Videos are converted with the colour rules
they were encoded with.
