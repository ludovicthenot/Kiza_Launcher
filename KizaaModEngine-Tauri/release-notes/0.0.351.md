Kiza Launcher 0.0.351

A new library, a moving background, and a calmer launcher.

## New

**Creating an instance, as a short sheet.** It says what will be made as
you choose; the loader is four tiles, the versions one list, and Java folds
away under Advanced options.

**Discover's project page is a rounded pane**, and lists fade under their
search bar instead of meeting it at a line.

**Instance settings and activity, rearranged.** Settings are one column of
grouped lists (General, Performance, Files, Kiza Client, Remove); Activity
has segmented tabs, a filterable log and the tools grouped at the foot.

**Servers, redrawn.** Segmented tabs, a frosted New Server card with its
fields as one list, and even preset cards lit by their own colour.

**One even shade over the waves**, from the title bar down, and the mod
list as a rounded card.

**A library like an album shelf.** Instances are compact pictures with
their name underneath; a large window shows more of them, not bigger ones.
Play is a round button on the picture.

**Violet waves behind the launcher.** A slow animated background in the
launcher's colours, paused when the window is hidden.

**The Kiza Manager, redrawn.** Segmented tabs, a grouped activity list and
the tools in one capsule.

**The chosen instance in a bar at the foot of the library**, with its facts,
its tools and Play, staying put while the library scrolls.

**Tooltips that match the launcher** on every button, fading in where they
stand.

**The title bar names the app**, with the channel as a small tag.

**Right-click an instance.** Play, Rename, Change the picture, Open folder,
Check, Export and Delete, from the library without opening it.

**Settings, redrawn.** Nine pages instead of twelve: Language moved into
General, Storage into Downloads, Connections into Advanced. Your account
sits at the top, every page has its coloured icon, and settings are
grouped lists with switches.

**Mod sheets.** A mod's details are one list under its own icon, and the
install window names every dependency, optional ones included, instead of
showing a number.

**A calmer instance header.** The name over one line of facts, the small
tools in one group, and a Mods toolbar of filled controls.

**Watch an install happen.** Installing a mod shows a bar, which file is
downloading, and a check on each one as it lands.

## Fixed

**Tooltips no longer slide in** from the corner of the window.

**The Kiza Manager window** no longer shows a scrollbar down its edge.

**No dark band under the Mods title** over the moving background.

**The folded sidebar's labels** show in front of the page, not behind it.

**The Mods title** fades into the search bar instead of ending on a line.

**1.12 instances show their picture.** A 1.12.2 card shows the version's
title-screen panorama instead of a green placeholder.

**The Minecraft and Java page** no longer runs off the side of the window.
