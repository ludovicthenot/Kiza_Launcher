Kiza Launcher 0.0.371

## Fixed

**Every video has its picture in the library.** Videos in H.265, AV1 or
VP9, and some H.264 ones, showed an empty tile: their thumbnail is now made
by the launcher's FFmpeg, which reads them all.

**A smooth blur behind the menus.** Over a video, the blur behind the
options and the other menus showed squares. It is now made at twice the
size and without visible compression; videos already prepared are prepared
again once, on their own.

**Several screens.** The automatic video quality follows the largest
screen connected, so taking the game from one screen to another no longer
prepares the video again or starts it over.
