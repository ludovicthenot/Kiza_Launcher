Kiza Launcher 0.0.378

## New

**Project pages, redesigned.** A project's page puts its picture behind
its name, the Install button beside it with every release one click away,
an overview with its pictures and its author's description, and the facts
on the side, a card each.

**A page for your installed mods.** Click a mod in an instance's list and
it opens whole: its pictures, its description and links, and what the
instance holds of it: the release on disk, its files, the switch, the
folder.

**A better gallery.** Arrows at each end that still has more, a slim bar
to drag, and a film strip under the picture you opened.

**Modpacks show their progress.** Making an instance from a modpack says
what it is doing and how many mods are left.

## Fixed

- A modpack from Modrinth now lists its mods in the instance's Mods tab,
  with their switch, their page and their updates. Instances made before
  this get their mods listed the first time the tab opens.
- Modrinth modpacks install several times faster.
- Mods installed from Modrinth or CurseForge remember which project they
  are, so their page and their updates find them.
- CurseForge shows every release of a modpack, whatever instance you are in.
- Notifications speak your language.
- Pictures load lighter and the pages scroll more smoothly.
