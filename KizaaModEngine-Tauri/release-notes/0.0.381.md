Kiza Launcher 0.0.381

## Fixed

- **No more hidden buttons beside the title menu.** A mod's icon could sit
  exactly on Minecraft's accessibility button at some window sizes and hide
  it. Small buttons now follow the button they were next to, outside the
  column, and stand side by side when two want the same place.
- **Essential's menus are left as Essential draws them.** The wardrobe,
  the social menu, the pictures and the rest keep their own look; only
  Essential's settings take the Kiza style.
