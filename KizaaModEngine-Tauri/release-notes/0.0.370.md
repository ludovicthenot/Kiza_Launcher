Kiza Launcher 0.0.370

## New

**A library of menu backgrounds.** Every image, GIF and video you add to
the Kiza Client menu is kept, with a thumbnail, in one library shared by
all your instances. Pick one in a click, add another with the + tile or by
dropping a file on the window, and take one out with the cross on its
tile. The backgrounds you had chosen before move into it on their own,
videos with the frames already prepared.

**Every kind of video for the menu.** The launcher now carries its own
FFmpeg, reduced to reading video, and the Kiza Client prepares menu videos
with it: H.264 from any encoder (videos from NVIDIA's NVEnc no longer leave
coloured trails), H.265/HEVC, AV1, VP9 and ProRes, in MP4, MOV, MKV and
WebM. Videos filmed in HDR, as phones do, are brought down to normal
colours instead of looking grey and washed out.

**A lighter menu video.** Measured beside a game-like load:
- A 1080p 60 fps video now costs the game 4 % of its frame rate instead of
  20 %, and its 1 % low stays at 115 fps instead of dropping to 63. Each
  frame goes to the graphics card from where it was decoded, with no copy.
- Preparing a video takes 7 seconds instead of 23, and the prepared frames
  take a third less room on disk.
- On a small processor, preparing a video no longer makes the game stutter.
- Nothing is decoded while the game is minimized.
- The automatic quality now goes a step down by itself, and remembers it,
  when a video cannot keep up on your computer. Choosing a quality again
  puts it back.

## Fixed

- On a second screen, or on an ultrawide one, the automatic video quality
  now follows the screen the game is actually on, and how wide it is.
- Videos filmed on a phone held upright play upright.
- Videos that do not say which colours they use are shown in the right ones.
