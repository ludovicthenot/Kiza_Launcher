Kiza Launcher 0.0.366

Fixes for the menu video.

## Fixed

**"The menu video could not be prepared".** Choosing a video while
another was shown restarted its preparation on every frame of the copy,
and the preparations got in each other's way. A video is now copied in
whole before it is used, and prepared once.

**Measurements over an image.** The on-screen measurements are a video
setting, and now show only behind a video.
