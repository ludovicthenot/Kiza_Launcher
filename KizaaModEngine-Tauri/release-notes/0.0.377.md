Kiza Launcher 0.0.377

## New

**Find a modpack.** "New instance" has a button that opens every modpack
on Modrinth and CurseForge, searched, sorted and filtered like the rest of
Discover. Choose one and its version, and it becomes your instance.

**Pictures of every project.** Mods, modpacks, shaders and packs show
their screenshots under their description; open one to see it whole and
go through them with the arrows. The picture the author chose sits behind
the project's name.

**A page for every project.** The button beside the heart opens a
project whole: its pictures, its author's full description, how to
install it, the Minecraft versions and loaders it supports, and its
links.
