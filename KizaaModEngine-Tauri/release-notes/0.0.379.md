Kiza Launcher 0.0.379

## Fixed

- **Buttons other mods add to the title screen are back.** Flashback,
  ReplayMod, Essential and the like put their own buttons on Minecraft's
  menu; in the Kiza Client they could be clicked but not seen, or showed
  up in the grid under names like <ESSENTIAL_SOCIAL>. Each now stays
  exactly where its mod put it, drawn in Kiza's style with words you can
  read. Minecraft's language and accessibility buttons show again too.
- **No more checkerboard behind the menu.** The first launch of a new
  instance could show a magenta and black checkerboard in place of the
  Kiza background. The background now always loads.
