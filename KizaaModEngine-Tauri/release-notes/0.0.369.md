Kiza Launcher 0.0.369

## New

**Your own accent colour.** A sixth swatch, a ring of every hue, opens a
palette in the Kiza Client settings: pick the saturation and brightness in
the square and the hue in the strip under it. Buttons, switches and the
menu follow it as you drag.

**Kiza's buttons on mods' screens.** With Kiza style everywhere on, Mod
Menu and a mod's own settings, such as Distant Horizons', get Kiza's
buttons too. A mod's icon buttons are left as they are, and coloured
labels keep their colour.

## Fixed

**The new logo on the taskbar.** 0.0.368 still carried the old icon inside
the program; this one carries the new one.

**Videos in a format the menu cannot play.** An H.265 (HEVC), AV1 or VP9
video is now refused as soon as it is chosen, with its format named and a
way out: convert it to H.264, with HandBrake for instance.
