Kiza Launcher 0.0.383

## New

- **Servers, redesigned.** Your servers are a list on the left, grouped by
  running and stopped, with "New server" on top; the server you choose opens
  beside it on a banner in its preset's colours. Creating a server starts from
  a banner where you type its name, then the preset, then the world; memory,
  Java and the port are folded under one line.
- **Groups.** Hold Ctrl and click instances to choose several, then group
  them or delete them together. A group is a folder in the library, like a
  Discord folder: it opens into a capsule holding its instances.
- **Drag your instances.** Press and hold a card to move it anywhere in the
  library (the order is kept), to drop it on another card to make a group, or
  to throw it in the trash that rises from the bottom of the window.
- **Modpacks bring their logo.** A modpack installed from the catalogue gets
  its own logo as the instance's picture, and its logo flies from the
  catalogue to its new card in the library.
- **Instances and Host slide** from one to the other like pages, and switching
  back no longer reloads the page.
- **Quieter scrollbars**, as on macOS: thin, and only there while you scroll
  or point at the area.

## Fixed

- Modrinth modpacks now apply their client-only files (options, client
  configs, resource packs), which were left out.
- Deleting an instance no longer replays the whole library's entrance.
- Updating from 0.0.382 was refused by the installer, which did not recognise
  the licences file it had installed itself.
