Kiza Launcher 0.0.340

A tray menu of Kiza's own, a launcher that sleeps when it is hidden, and
a page that draws only what changed.

## New

**The tray menu is Kiza's.** Right-click on the tray icon opens a small
window the launcher draws itself — your account, Open, the last instance
with a Play button, the servers that are up, updates, Settings, Quit —
in place of the system's two-line list.

## Changed

**A hidden launcher sleeps.** Closed to the tray, the launcher's page is
suspended the way a background tab is, and Windows is asked for its
memory back: about a third of what it held, and no processor time at
all, until the window comes back.

**The skin stand draws on demand.** It redrew sixty times a second
whether or not anything moved. It now draws once for a still view, at
twenty-four while the figure turns, and not at all while the window is
hidden. Front is the default; Orbit is a click away.

**Told, not asked.** Which games are running is announced the moment it
changes, instead of asked every three seconds by every page that cared.
The General page's own commands are off the thread that draws the window.

**Notifications carry the icon, and say one line.** Windows draws a
toast's icon from a registered image file; it was given the executable
instead and drew nothing. The tray notice is one sentence now.

**A skin address is HTTPS and public.** Nothing on this machine or the
network behind it can be fetched by typing its address into the skin page.
