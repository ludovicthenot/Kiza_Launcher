Kiza Launcher 0.0.355

A lighter launcher: smoother, and holding less memory.

## New

**Each loader shows its own mark.** Minecraft, Fabric, Forge and NeoForge
logos on the library cards, the instance header, the sidebar, the servers
and the create sheet.

**Microsoft's mark** wherever an account or a sign-in is Microsoft's: the
title bar, the account menu, Settings, first launch and the Kiza Manager.

## Faster

**No more freezes while the launcher works.** Reading the mod list, backing
up a world, loading the library's pictures and the status checks no longer
run on the thread that draws the window.

**Smoother at rest.** The waves draw their own glow instead of the page
blurring the whole window on every frame, and the large panels over them no
longer re-blur it either. The waves also hold still while another window
has the focus, a game most often.

**Long mod lists stay quick.** Rows out of view are not drawn, and typing
in the search no longer waits on the list.

## Lighter

**Instance pictures at the size they are shown.** A 1.21 panorama went to
the page as about 800 KB; it is now about 50 KB.

**A minimised launcher hands memory back** and still announces a finished
download.

**The tray menu lets go of its memory** after two minutes unopened.
