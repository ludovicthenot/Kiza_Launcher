Kiza Launcher 0.0.359

A new title menu inside the game, and settings that do what they say.

## New

**The Kiza menu in Minecraft.** The title screen now opens on the Kiza
emblem and a grid of buttons: Singleplayer and Multiplayer across, the rest
paired below, each with its icon. Buttons fade to violet under the pointer,
and the emblem lights up when you point at it. Every other Minecraft screen —
pause, options, worlds, servers — uses the same buttons.

**NeoForge gets the Kiza client.** Instances on NeoForge now run the Kiza
client like Fabric and Forge ones do.

**Imported themes.** Appearance can import a `.kizatheme`, wear it and remove
it again.

## Fixed

**Settings.** "Offer to hunt the broken mod" after a crash now offers it. A
crash and a finished server backup now send their notification. Resetting the
settings keeps your channel and your read release notes. Units, dates and
times follow your choice everywhere.

**Appearance.** Text size reaches every label, density changes the spacing of
lists, and the corner radius follows the theme until you move it.

**No more flashing terminal** when opening General, or while Forge, NeoForge
or a server installs.
