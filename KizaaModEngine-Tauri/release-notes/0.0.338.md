Kiza Launcher 0.0.338

Change the skin on your Microsoft account from the launcher, and a
front page redrawn: the title bar, the toolbar and the instance footer.

## New

**Your skin, changed for real.** Settings → Skins puts the account's skin
on a 3D stand — turn it, zoom, see it from the front, the side, the back.
Bring a PNG from disk or from a web address, look at it on the stand, and
apply it: the change goes through Minecraft's own profile service, so the
game and every other launcher see it. Classic or slim arms are read from
the image and can be corrected. The capes your account owns are there
too, one at a time or none, and one click puts the default skin back.

**A skin library.** Skins saved by name — the one you wear, or one you
are only looking at — and put back with a click. Every image is checked
before it goes anywhere: a PNG, 64x64 or the old 64x32, and small.

**Filter, on the front page.** Order by last played or by name, and show
only one loader's instances. The search is always on the page, and Ctrl+K
lands the cursor in it.

## Changed

**The title bar.** The version, your account, Settings and the window
buttons share one shape and one height.

**The toolbar.** Instances | Host with an icon each, the search in the
middle, Filter, Import and Create at the right — the same row on both
front pages.

**The instance footer.** The picture, the name and the meta line, then
last played, Java, the folder and the status as tiles, then Open folder,
Edit and More.
