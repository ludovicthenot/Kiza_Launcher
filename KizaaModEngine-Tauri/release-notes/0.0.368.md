Kiza Launcher 0.0.368

The client settings, in your language and out of the way.

## New

**Client settings in the game's language.** The settings follow the
language set in Minecraft, in 17 languages, and a resource pack can
translate them like any other text of the game. Russian, Serbian,
Ukrainian, Chinese, Japanese and Korean labels, Kiza's and Minecraft's, no
longer show as empty boxes.

**A drawer from the left edge.** A tab halfway down the left of the title
screen slides the settings out, narrow and translucent, with the menu
still in view beside them. The same tab closes them.

## Changed

**Automatic video quality** picks 1440p only on processors with eight
cores or more.

## Fixed

**"Sync required" and "Last checked" on the Mods page.** An instance with
a disabled mod always said it needed a sync, and the time shown was that
of the instance's last change of state, days old. It now says synced when
every active mod is in place and none of the disabled ones is, and the
time is that of the last sync or update check.

## Changed

**Back from the catalogues to what you browse.** While browsing resource
packs, shaders or data packs, the sidebar's first row now goes back to the
installed ones of that kind, with their count -- on Vanilla instances too.
It used to go back to mods, or, without mods, not be there at all.

**No more KizaClient resource pack where Kiza draws the menu.** On Fabric,
Forge and NeoForge from 1.17, the client draws the title menu itself, so
the branding pack is removed from the instance and its list. Vanilla and
older versions keep it.

**Instance folders named after their instance.** In
`minecraft/instances`, each instance's folder now carries its name --
`serv entre anglola`, `Kiza test` -- instead of an id, with a number when
two share one: `Kiza test (2)`. Renaming an instance renames its folder;
while the game is running, the folder follows at the next start of the
launcher. Existing folders are renamed the first time this version starts.

**The new Kiza Launcher logo** -- the glowing K on its night-sky tile -- on
the app icon, the taskbar and tray, the title bar, the installers and the
About, startup and update screens.
