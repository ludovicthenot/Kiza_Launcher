Kiza Launcher 0.0.374

## New

**A new loading screen in the game.** The Kiza emblem and "Kiza Client",
what is being prepared, a rounded bar in your accent colour with its
percentage, and what the game is doing, over a soft violet light. Once
the game has loaded, the bar and the words step aside and the emblem and
the name rise to their place on the title screen.

**On Forge and NeoForge too.** Their own window still shows for the first
moments, before any mod is loaded; Kiza's screen takes over from there.

## Fixed

**The loading screen moves smoothly.** The emblem no longer flickers or
steps from one frame to the next, and no longer disappears for an instant
when the title screen takes over.
