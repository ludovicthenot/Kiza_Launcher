Kiza Launcher 0.0.361

Every Minecraft options page in the Kiza style, sliders included.

## Changed

**Options pages.** Video Settings, Music & Sounds, Controls and the other
options pages now use Kiza's buttons as well.

**Sliders.** Volume, field of view and every other slider fill with violet up
to their value, with a thin handle and the setting's name over it.

**The name.** "Kiza Client" now uses its own rounded lettering: under the
emblem on the title screen, beside it in the pause menu.
