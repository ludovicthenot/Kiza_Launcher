Kiza Launcher 0.0.344

The launcher that plays RLCraft, and says who is talking.

## Channels

**The Alpha has closed.** Thank you to everyone who tested it. An Alpha
launcher says goodbye and uninstalls itself; your instances, worlds,
accounts and skins stay on your computer for the Kiza Beta.

**Stable is now Beta.** A Stable launcher switches to the Beta channel on
its own and says so. Nothing to reinstall.

## New

**A new look.** Discover is a list and a page instead of a table of boxes,
and the pack's own icon colours its page. The instance sidebar folds down
to its icons (Ctrl+B). Buttons are filled shapes, notifications are frosted
glass, and the title bar no longer ends on a line.

**Install packs from their page.** Resource packs, data packs and shaders
each have a Find button that opens the catalogue on that kind; importing a
.zip is still there for files you already have.

**Switching a mod off really switches it off.** The file is renamed to
.jar.disabled the moment you click, whatever put it there, and renamed back
when you switch it on.

**Stop game closes the game.** It asks the window to close, as the cross
would, even while the game is still loading: the world saves and no pack
mistakes it for a crash.

**Import from another launcher.** Import → From another launcher lists what
the CurseForge App, the Modrinth App, Prism, MultiMC and the Minecraft
Launcher have on this computer, with version, loader, mods and worlds read
from their own records, and copies what you tick. Nothing changes there.

**Any archive imports.** A pack or export as .zip, .mrpack, .7z, .rar,
.tar, .tar.gz, .tar.xz or .tar.bz2. A .mrpack on disk installs like one
from the catalogue.

**A check-up for every instance.** Management → Check-up verifies the game
files, the native libraries and every mod against the catalogue, fetches
again what it knows the origin of, and names what needs a hand.

**Crash reports, if you want them.** After a crash the launcher can send
what the doctor found, what the launch was and the end of the log — ask
each time, always, or never. One report per kind of crash per day.

**The Kiza Manager, rebuilt.** A header with the instance, version, loader,
profile, account, Java and memory; one line that moves with the game and
the warnings counted beside it; a Problem tab that names the line the game
went down on and hands over to the doctor; a log that filters by level and
by who is talking — Kiza Launcher, Kiza Client, or the game — and
searches. The latest.log button works.

## Fixed

**The doctor can be undone.** Every fix — the doctor's and the compatibility
fixer's — takes a restore point first and waits for the next launch; if the
game crashes again, one click puts the instance back.

**Three more crashes read.** An empty natives folder; a mod that is not the
release its neighbours were built against (found by the package in the
stack frame); a mod choking on another mod's content because that one was
switched off — which turns "disable Bountiful" into "switch Switch-Bow back
on".

**Stop game is a stop.** It no longer reads as a crash.

**The nearest build is the one after the pin.** When Modrinth never had the
build a pack pinned, the release just after it is taken, not the one
before: mods add far more often than they remove.

**A light copy of a pack is told apart from the release.** An imported
CurseForge pack a third the size of the official file is named for what it
is, and the official pack offered in its place.

**Blocked mods have a button.** "I have the file" in the missing-mods window
checks the file against the pack and catalogues it.

**Kiza stands aside for your mods.** A pack with CustomMainMenu keeps its
menu, one with Borderless Mining keeps its window, one with CraftPresence
keeps its Discord status. The branding pack sits at the bottom of the
resource pack pile now, not the top.

## Under the hood

Every launcher line says `Kiza Launcher`, every client line says `Kiza
Client`, and a launch opens its log with what it is. The library appears
a third sooner; a CurseForge pack downloads six mods at a time with its
metadata in two requests. The crate root is a table of contents.
