Kiza Launcher 0.0.358

Themes can now change far more of the launcher.

## New

**Themes reach further.** A theme can choose the launcher's font and how
heavy page titles are, hold animations still or change their pace, put the
instance sidebar on the right, and set how wide library cards are and how
far apart they sit.

**Hover and focus, themed.** Buttons, fields and sidebar rows can take the
theme's own colours under the pointer and while typing, and cards can lift.

**Decorations.** A theme can scatter up to twelve pictures or GIFs over the
launcher — snow, a garland, a pumpkin — on every page or just one. They
never get in the way of a click.

**Words and icons.** A theme can rename some buttons and titles and give
them other icons.

**Kiza Maker: drag, stack and detach.** Elements are dragged straight into
place, brought forward or sent back like in a drawing program
(Ctrl+] and Ctrl+[), and moved earlier or later in their row. The editor
can open in its own window, so the launcher is seen at its own size.
